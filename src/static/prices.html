Electricity price

The price per kWh, in cents (for example 30.5 for 30.5 c€/kWh), puts a cost on charge sessions and energy, on the Sessions and Energy pages and in the CSV downloads. Each price is valid from its start date until the next price starts, so when the price changes, add a new one: earlier energy keeps its old price. The earliest price also applies to everything before its date. Without any price, no costs are shown.
